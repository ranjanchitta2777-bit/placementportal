About Placement Preparation Portal

A Complete Platform for Students to Learn, Prepare, Build Resumes and Get Placed in Top Companies.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between students and recruiters by providing one platform for placement preparation, coding practice, aptitude learning, resume building, and company recruitment.

What We Offer

Aptitude Preparation

Practice Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning and Verbal Ability with company-specific questions.

Coding Practice

Improve your Data Structures, Algorithms and Programming skills for placement interviews.

Resume Builder

Build a professional ATS-friendly resume and download it anytime.

Company Drives

Explore the latest placement drives from top companies and apply easily.

Student Dashboard

Manage profile, upload resume, track applications, and prepare efficiently.

Admin Dashboard

Manage students, companies, applications, and recruitment process.
